Guided Onboarding

Up and running in 4 simple steps.

No database provisioning, no complex server installations. Google handles the identity and storage automatically.

01

Google 1-Click Verification

Authenticate via the Google Account Chooser. Your identity is cryptographically verified without requiring passwords or activation emails.

02

Provision Organization

Enter your brand name and industry sector. The system automatically initializes 6 isolated database workbooks inside Google Drive.

Add your inventory SKUs, configure unit costs, minimum warning thresholds, and map suppliers and bin storage locations.

Record purchase deliveries, issue materials to sales counters, execute branch transfers, and export audit-ready Excel reports.

Enterprise Multi-Tenant Security

Your data is strictly yours. Zero cross-tenant leakage.

Zolexora IMS employs two distinct layers of physical and logical isolation. The root Google Drive folder is completely private to the platform administrator. Every tenant’s queries are strictly filtered by their authenticated Organization ID.
